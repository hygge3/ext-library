快速开始

只需几行配置，即可开始使用 ext-library

pom.xml 
<dependencyManagement>
    <dependencies>
        <dependency>
            <groupId>ext.library</groupId>
            <artifactId>ext-bom</artifactId>
            <version>4.0.0</version>
            <type>pom</type>
            <scope>import</scope>
        </dependency>
    </dependencies>
</dependencyManagement>

<dependencies>
    <dependency>
        <groupId>ext.library</groupId>
        <artifactId>ext-core</artifactId>
    </dependency>
</dependencies>
